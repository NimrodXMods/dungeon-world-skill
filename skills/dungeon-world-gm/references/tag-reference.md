# Tag Reference (Official Glossary)

The book's own appendix - the single authoritative definition for every tag used across equipment, monsters, and steadings. Cross-references: equipment tags also live in [equipment-and-services](references/equipment-and-services.md), monster tags in [treasure-and-monster-building](references/treasure-and-monster-building.md), steading tags in [npc-tools](references/npc-tools.md) - this file is the complete alphabetical version if you need to look one up fast without knowing which category it's in.

**Equipment**: n Ammo (ammo stock, not exact count) - Applied (only useful carefully applied to a person/food/drink) - n Armor (subtract n from damage; only highest n counts if multiple) - +n Armor (adds to total, stacks) - Awkward (unwieldy) - n Coins (price) - +n Damage (add n when dealing damage) - Dangerous (consequences for careless use) - Forceful (can knock back/off feet) - Ignores Armor (armor doesn't reduce this damage) - Messy (destructively rips apart) - n Piercing (subtract n from target's armor for this attack) - Precise (DEX instead of STR to Hack & Slash) - Ration (edible; consumes on Make Camp) - Reload (takes more than a moment to reset) - Requires (only usable by those meeting the requirement) - Slow (takes more than a moment to use) - Stun (stun damage instead of normal) - Thrown (still dangerous once thrown) - Touch (used by touching the target's skin) - Two-handed - n Weight (counts against Load) - Worn (must be worn to use) - n Uses (limited uses). Clumsy is equipment-specific: -1 ongoing while worn.

**Weapon range**: Hand (arm's length or closer) - Close (arm's reach plus a foot or two) - Reach (several feet, up to ~10) - Near (whites-of-their-eyes range) - Far (shouting distance).

**Monster**: Amorphous (bizarre/unnatural anatomy) - Cautious (prefers defense, avoids unwinnable fights) - Construct (made, not born) - Devious (its real threat isn't a straight fight) - Hoarder (collects valuables; bonus on random treasure rolls) - Intelligent (person-smart) - Magical (beyond physical law) - Organized (formal group structure; killing one may draw revenge) - Planar (not of this world) - Stealthy (avoids detection, attacks by surprise) - Terrifying (proximity alone can break morale).
Monster organization: Group (3-6) - Horde (7+, often many more) - Solitary (lives/fights alone; bonus on random treasure rolls).
Monster size: Tiny (smaller than a halfling) - Small (halfling-sized) - Large (bigger than human, up to cart-sized) - Huge (small-house-sized or bigger).

**Steading**: Arcane (notable arcane power lives here) - Blight (recurring problem, often a monster infestation) - Craft (known for mastery of the listed craft) - Divine (notable religious org/person resides here) - Dwarven / Elven (mostly or entirely that ancestry) - Enmity (bad blood with the listed steadings) - Exotic (goods/services unavailable elsewhere nearby) - Guild (the listed guild holds sway) - History (historically significant) - Lawless (rampant crime) - Market (regular public market; +1 to Supply rolls) - Militia/Watch/Guard/Garrison/Battalion/Legion/None (defense tiers, ascending) - Need (can't survive without the listed resource) - Oath (owes fealty/defense to the listed steadings) - Personage (a named important person lives here) - Power (holds sway in an arena - arcane/divine/political) - Religion (the listed religion has a strong hold) - Resource (consistent access to the listed resource) - Safe (no trouble unless players bring it) - Trade (regular trade with the listed steading).
Steading prosperity: Dirt (nothing for sale) - Poor (bare essentials) - Moderate (common items) - Wealthy (almost any mundane item) - Rich (any mundane item + stranger stuff).
Steading population: Exodus (near-collapse) - Shrinking - Steady - Growing - Booming (far more people than space).
