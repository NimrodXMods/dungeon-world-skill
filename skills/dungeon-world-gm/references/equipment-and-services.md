# Equipment, Services, and Prices

Whenever creativity is needed use `idea_gen.py` to avoid repitition from priors.

When new equipment types are needed that are not an exact match, attempt to interpolate coin price from comparable items below. Adjust price by -50% for very poor quality, add +25% to +100% for better than average quality. High quality higher cost items should have a meaningful benefit of some sort (beneficial tag etc.) above the standard, especially ones that cost +50% to +100% more.

## Equipment Tag Glossary

General: Applied (only works carefully applied to a person/food/drink) - Awkward (unwieldy) - +Bonus (a specific situational modifier) - Dangerous (GM may freely invoke consequences of careless handling) - Ration (edible) - Requires (works poorly/not at all without meeting a requirement) - Slow (takes minutes+) - Touch (applied by touching the target's skin) - Two-handed - n weight (100 standard coins = 1 weight; gems/art may differ; unlisted weight = not meant to be carried) - Worn (must be worn to use) - n Uses.
Weapon-specific: n Ammo (counts as ammo stock, not individual arrows) - Forceful (can knock back/off feet) - +n Damage - Ignores Armor (target's armor doesn't reduce it) - Messy (destructively rips apart) - n Piercing (subtracts n from target's armor for that attack) - Precise (Hack & Slash with DEX instead of STR) - Reload (takes more than a moment to reset) - Stun (stun damage instead of normal) - Thrown (once thrown it's gone - can't choose to spend ammo instead on a 7-9 Volley).
Armor-specific: n Armor (only the single highest-value armor item counts, non-stacking) - +n Armor (stacks with other armor) - Clumsy (-1 ongoing while worn, cumulative across multiple clumsy items).

## Consumable In-Use Effects (not just price/weight)

- Bandages (3 uses, slow): a few minutes of bandaging heals 4 damage, spends a use.
- Poultices and Herbs (2 uses, slow): careful treatment heals 7 damage, spends a use.
- Healing Potion: drink the whole thing, heal 10 damage OR remove one debility (your choice).
- Keg of Dwarven Stout: shared, +1 to a Carouse roll; drunk solo, you're very drunk.
- Bag of Books (5 uses): if it happens to contain the right book for your Spout Lore subject, spend a use for +1 to the roll.
- Antitoxin: drink to cure one poison currently affecting you.
- Halfling Pipeleaf (6 uses): share it (spend 2 uses) for +1 forward to Parley with that person.
- Adventuring Gear (5 uses): rummage it for any useful mundane item (chalk, poles, spikes, rope, etc.), spend a use, no roll needed.

## Equipment Tags

- **Applied**: consumed/inhaled by target
- **Awkward**: unwieldy
- **Dangerous**: GM may freely invoke consequences of careless use
- **Ration**: edible
- **Requires**: needs a condition met to work
- **Slow**: takes minutes+ to use
- **Touch**: used by touching to skin
- **Forceful**: can knock someone back/off feet
- **Messy**: destructively rips things apart
- **Precise**: Hack & Slash with DEX instead of STR
- **Reload**: takes a beat to reset after use
- **Stun**: stun damage instead of normal
- **Clumsy**: -1 ongoing cumulative while worn
