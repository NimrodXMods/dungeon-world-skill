# Follower Moves ("Lead the Way")

Use `npc_gen.py` to create followers. This is for moves and reference.

Source: *The Perilous Wilds* (Revised Edition), "Lead the Way" chapter by Jeremy
Strandberg, p22-24 - CC BY-SA 3.0. Replaces the core rulebook's basic hireling
rules (see [npc-tools](references/npc-tools.md) for those).

Followers make the standard Encumbrance, Make Camp, and Recover moves, and Last
Breath at the GM's discretion. Otherwise, they don't use the basic moves - use
the moves below instead.

## Recruit

When you go looking to hire help, tell the GM what you're offering and whom
you're looking for: **a group of ___** (porters, guards, minstrels, angry
farmers, etc.) or **a skilled ___** (guide, sage, burglar, bodyguard, etc.). A
group is a follower like any other, just with the group tag.

If the GM says you can't find that kind of help here, start over or move on.
Otherwise roll+nothing (+1 with a good reputation in these parts):

- 10+: they're yours for the hiring
- 7-9: GM chooses 1: they demand greater compensation (coin or otherwise) /
  no one here fits the bill, but you hear of someone elsewhere who does / they
  have a need that must be met first (permission, a favor, etc.) / you can
  tell at a glance they're less than ideal (ask the GM how)
- 6-: no one shows, mark XP

GM chooses or rolls specifics (Quality, Loyalty, Instinct, Cost, tags, etc.)
as needed - to be discovered through play, possibly by grilling them in the
fiction right then.

### Follower stats (reference - only matters once you've actually recruited one)

Like monsters: HP, Armor, Damage, and an Instinct, usually with moves and/or
tags for what they're good at and how they act. Unlike monsters, three more:

- **Quality** (-1 rubbish to +3 masterful; 0 = average competence): how
  effective they are, particularly within their tags/moves.
- **Loyalty** (usually starts +1; ranges -2 to +3): their commitment to you
  and your cause. A resource the GM can spend down. At -3, they betray or
  abandon you (see Watch Them Go, below).
- **Cost**: what motivates them to follow - usually coin/material reward,
  sometimes a strong leader or cause instead; not always a tangible thing.
  Paying it raises Loyalty (see Pay Up, below).

Followers deal and take damage like monsters, at the GM's discretion or per a
move: dealing damage rolls their damage die; taking damage costs HP. At 0 HP
they're out of the action - dead, dying, or otherwise, per the GM.

## Order Follower

When you order or expect a follower to do something dangerous, degrading, or
contrary to their Instinct, roll+Loyalty:

- 10+: they do it, now
- 7-9: they do it, but GM chooses 1: -1 Loyalty / they complain loudly, now
  or later, and demand something in return / caution, laziness, or fear makes
  them slow to get it done
- 6-: mark XP, and the GM makes a move

## Do Their Thing

When a follower does something chancy within the scope of their tags or
moves, roll+Quality:

- 10+: they do it, as well as one could reasonably hope
- 7-9: they do it, but there's an unforeseen cost, consequence, or limitation
  (ask the GM what)
- 6-: mark XP, and the GM makes a move

When a follower does something chancy *beyond* the scope of their tags or
moves, or acts on their own initiative, the GM just says what happens - no
roll for this move.

*Example (paraphrased from the book): a Ranger orders her cougar companion
(Quality +1) to harry a spellcaster mid-fight. She rolls 9, +1 Quality = 10+:
the cougar does it "as well as one could reasonably hope" - but the GM knows
the target is tough enough to survive, so narrates the cougar driving it off
rather than landing a killing blow.*

## Call for Assistance

When a follower helps you make a move that calls for a roll, take +1 to that
roll - but they're exposed to any consequences that follow.

- Hack and Slash or Volley: roll their damage die alongside yours, use the
  higher result, and add your own damage bonuses on top.
- Defend: spend 1 hold to redirect an attack to them instead of yourself.

## Pay Up

When you pay a follower's Cost, increase their Loyalty by 1 (max +3). Can't
retrigger this move until you and the follower have Made Camp together.

## Watch Them Go

When a follower has -3 Loyalty, they betray or abandon you at the next
opportunity.

## Follower Tags

Every follower's first tag is always a **___-wise** (fill in the blank with
whatever the follower knows best - e.g. woods-wise, sea-wise, herb-wise):
they can roll +Quality to Spout Lore about it on your behalf. Additional
tags come from this list:

- **Archer**: effective ranged attacks (not necessarily bows) - roll +Quality to Volley on your behalf.
- **Athletic**: good at running, jumping, climbing, and the like.
- **Beautiful**: makes an impression, draws admiration and attention.
- **Cautious**: careful and methodical; avoids acting rashly.
- **Connected (___)**: has friends or contacts in a steading or group.
- **Cunning**: clever and observant - roll +Quality to Discern Realities on your behalf.
- **Devious**: deceitful, treacherous, tricky - just the sort you want working for you.
- **Group**: a team, gang, or mob, with all the strengths and disadvantages of greater numbers.
- **Guide (___)**: knows a particular steading or region - roll +Quality to Spout Lore, Scout Ahead, or Navigate for the party there.
- **Hardy**: tough, hard-working, tolerates discomfort - +2 Load.
- **Healer**: tends the injured and sick - add Quality to damage healed when tending wounds with bandages or herbs & poultice.
- **Meek**: accepts their lowly lot - does degrading/unreasonable things without needing Order Follower.
- **Magical**: magical by nature or training, sensitive to magic's workings - use at least one of their moves to elaborate on their powers.
- **Organized**: makes and follows plans well, works well in a group - once they agree to a plan, no Order Followers needed unless it goes south.
- **Self-sufficient**: can live off the land - roll +Quality to Forage.
- **Stealthy**: avoids detection, often gets the drop on foes.
- **Warrior**: +1 to their damage die roll; no Order Follower needed to get them to fight (unless the opposition is terrifying or overwhelming).
