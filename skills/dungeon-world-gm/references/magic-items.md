# Magic Items

Design note from the source: magic items should have interesting, setting-flavored effects - not just a flat mundane bonus like "+1 damage." Use these as ready-to-hand rewards, or as templates for homebrewing your own. Use `idea_gen.py` to help create a custom one or use below.

Always run `idea_gen.py std-magicitem` 1+ times and match output to one of these, or pick based on a combination of that and situational theme.

Look these up in rulebook-digest if more details needed!

- **Argo-Thaan, Holy Avenger** (Close, 2wt) - in a Paladin's hands: d12 damage die, access to every Paladin move, harms Evil creatures regardless of their defenses (which suffer agony merely touching it). Anyone else: just an awkward heavy sword. Drawn toward causes of true Good.
- **Arrows of Acheron** (1 ammo, 1wt) - never miss even blind/in total darkness; disintegrate if touched by sunlight.
- **Axe of the Conqueror-King** (Close, 1wt) - all your hirelings get +1 Loyalty regardless of your actual leadership.
- **Barb of the Black Gate** (0wt) - hammered into a corpse, permanently prevents that corpse from ever being raised again.
- **Bag of Holding** (0wt, infinite storage) - roll+WIS to retrieve an item: 10+ instant, 7-9 choose slow-but-exact or instant-but-GM-substituted.
- **The Burning Wheel** (2wt) - speak a god's name + roll+CON: 7+ that god grants an audience; 10+ you choose which stat drops a modifier tier as the price, 7-9 the GM chooses.
- **Captain Bligh's Cornucopia** (1wt) - blown horn produces a full meal for everyone who hears it.
- **The Carcosan Spire** (Reach, Thrown, 3wt) - ordinary spear vs. normal targets; can actually wound things otherwise immune to mundane weapons, and the wielder recognizes such targets on sight.
- **Cloak of Silent Stars** (1wt) - Defy Danger with any stat you like by narrating how the cloak "breaks the rules"; usable once per stat before its magic is spent.
- **Coin of Remembering** (0wt, one-shot) - redeem to instantly learn one forgotten fact (not necessarily forgotten by you); on failure, still shows a vision of who/what remembers it.
- **Common Scroll** (1 use, 0wt) - inscribed spell must be on your class's list or castable by you; cast it directly, no roll needed.
- **Devilsbane Oil** (1 use, 0wt) - applied to a weapon, a hit on an outer-planar creature undoes the magic binding/controlling it (may banish or just free it); also repels such creatures from a marked doorway/circle for a day.
- **Earworm Wax** (1 use, 0wt) - drip in a target's ear for 3 hold; spend hold to force one truthful answer per question.
- **The Echo** (0wt, one-shot) - unstoppering it grants a warning vision of a coming danger; afterward, at any point, force a reroll of any single die (yours or another's); gone after use.
- **The Epoch Lens** (1wt) - looking at an object through it reveals who made it and its origin.
- **Farsight Stone** (1wt) - name a location + roll+WIS: 10+ clear maintainable vision; 7-9 vision, but you draw the attention of something else watching through a linked stone.
- **The Fiasco Codex** (0wt, one-shot per reader) - roll+WIS: 10+ ask 2, 7-9 ask 1, from: what's my greatest opportunity right now? / who can I betray for advantage? / who shouldn't I trust? Takes 2-3 hours to read.
- **Flask of Breath** (0wt) - eternally full of breathable air; bubbles underwater, lets you breathe through smoke, etc.
- **Folly Held Aloft / The Wax Wings** (1wt) - roll+DEX to fly: 10+ full controlled flight as long as desired; 7-9 aloft but short/erratic flight (your choice which); 6- aloft, but the landing (and everything between) is the GM's call.
- **Immovable Rod** (0wt) - button-toggled: fixes in place absolutely until pressed again.
- **Infinite Book** (1wt) - contains everything that ever was/is/will be; adds a 12+ tier to Spout Lore while consulting it: GM hands you a solution to your current problem.
- **Inspectacles** (0wt) - worn during Discern Realities, a 10+ lets you ask any 3 questions at all (not limited to the normal list).
- **The Ku'meh Maneuver** (1wt, one-shot read) - roll+INT on first read: 10+ hold 3, 7-9 hold 1; spend hold to remotely Aid a companion on any roll regardless of distance; on a miss the GM gets 1 hold to instead apply -2 to you or whoever took your advice.
- **Lamented Memento** (0wt, one-shot) - trade it for an automatic 10+ result on a Last Breath roll.
- **Lodestone Shield** (+1 armor, 1wt) - while Defending against metal-weapon attackers, spend 1 hold per target to disarm them; occasionally has loose coins stuck to it.
- **Map of the Last Patrol** (0wt) - marked with a group's blood, always shows their location as long as they stay within the map's bounds.
- **Ned's Head** (1wt) - once per night, ask "who has it in for me?" and get one name; vanishes if its owner dies.
- **Nightsider's Key** (0wt) - unlocks any door you don't belong behind; as long as you stay undetected and take nothing but memories, the intrusion is never discovered at all.
- **Sacred Herbs** (0wt, multi-use bundle) - smoke and focus on a subject, roll+WIS: 10+ clear useful vision, 7-9 vision but unclear/metaphorical, miss: GM asks what you fear most, answered honestly.
- **The Sartar Duck** (0wt) - while carried, makes you an exceptional storyteller understood by any audience regardless of language.
- **Tears of Annalise** (0wt, paired) - two swallowed by two different people bind them: strong emotions (sadness/loss/fear/desire) are felt by both, until one draws the other's blood.
- **Teleportation Room** (Slow) - name a location + roll+INT: 10+ arrive exactly there; 7-9 GM picks a safe nearby spot; miss: somewhere, and definitely not safe.
- **Timunn's Armor** (1 armor, 1wt) - blends into any context, wearer always seems fashionably dressed to onlookers.
- **Titus' Truthful Tallow** (0wt) - lit candle: anyone in its light can't lie when asked a direct question (may stay silent/dissemble instead).
- **Tricksy Rope** (1wt) - obeys simple verbal commands (coil, slack, come here).
- **The Sterling Hand** (0wt) - a silver prosthetic hand/weapon (Near range), can harm silver-vulnerable creatures.
- **Vellius's Gauntlets** (1wt) - can't be disarmed, won't fall from a rope/ladder involuntarily.
- **Violation Glaive** (Reach, 2wt) - on a 10+ Hack & Slash, may choose to deal damage + take a counterattack + inflict a chosen emotion (fear/reverence/trust/etc.) instead of the normal options.
- **Vorpal Sword** (Close, 3 piercing, 2wt) - on dealing damage, the enemy must permanently lose something of their choice (an item, an advantage, a limb).
