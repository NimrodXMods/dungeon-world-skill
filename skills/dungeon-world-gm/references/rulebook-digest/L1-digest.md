# L1 Digest — Dungeon World Core Rulebook

## Introduction (p6-17) {#s-001} (full-fetch)
Tags: [setting, flavor]
Pure flavor text: a page of setting tone, then a one-paragraph in-character blurb per class (Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Paladin, Ranger, Thief, Wizard) written as a second-person pep-talk selling that class's fantasy. No mechanics. Not worth digesting further - see the actual book if you want a specific class's blurb verbatim for table-talk flavor.

## Playing the Game — core conversation & dice (p17-19) {#s-002} (full-fetch)
Tags: [core-mechanics]
Confirms the fiction-first conversational model already in [[gm-agenda-principles-moves]]. One precise mechanical note not already captured elsewhere in this skill: **a character literally cannot narrate an action that would trigger a move without that move occurring** - e.g. you can't just say "I dash past the orc" without it being a Defy Danger; narration and mechanics are inseparable, not the GM's discretion to skip.

## Moves, dice, and equipment interaction (p19-22) {#s-003} (full-fetch)
Tags: [core-mechanics, equipment]
Standard 2d6+mod resolution (10+/7-9/6-, mark XP on a miss) - matches [[core-moves]]. One addition: **equipment can gate whether a move triggers at all**, not just modify its odds - e.g. no weapon means Hack & Slash can't trigger against a dragon's scales; a halberd may be too unwieldy to bring to bear on an adjacent foe. This is a fictional-positioning check the GM should actually apply, not flavor text.

## Damage rules (p22-25) {#s-004} (full-fetch)
Tags: [core-mechanics, damage]
Damage-by-severity (d4/d6/d8/d10) matches [[core-moves]]. **Multi-attacker damage formula corrected here** - see F-001. Also: "ignores armor" tag should be added for damage from something unusually large, or from magic/poison. Temporary/circumstantial cover works like worn armor (1 armor partial cover, 2 armor major cover). Damage is fictional first - it can be assigned without a move being rolled if the fiction demands it (falling in a pit), and losing a body part or being decapitated is not modeled as HP loss at all, it just happens. Stun damage doesn't reduce HP; instead the target is Defying Danger to do anything at all until they recover. `b[XdY]` / `w[XdY]` notation means roll multiple times and take the best/worst result respectively (used on some monster stat blocks).

## Healing & Death (p25-27) {#s-005} (full-fetch)
Tags: [core-mechanics, death]
Healing only comes from a move/item (varies) or rest (Make Camp / Recover - see F-002), and HP never exceeds max. At 0 HP: Last Breath immediately, no stat added to the roll. A stable character (survived Last Breath) sits at 0 HP, unconscious, alive; taking damage again forces another Last Breath. Death after a miss is permanent but resurrection is always narratively available - the GM sets a cost/condition, fulfilling it returns the character to life; the *Resurrection* spell just makes this easier, doesn't bypass GM say-so. A new character can always be made at level 1 regardless (a hireling stepping up, a vengeful relative, etc.) while resurrection is pending or declined.

## Debilities (p27-28) {#s-006} (full-fetch)
Tags: [core-mechanics]
Matches [[core-moves]] exactly (Weak/Shaky/Sick/Stunned/Confused/Scarred, -1 to the tied stat's modifier not the score, can't stack the same debility twice, harder to heal than HP - safe rest for days, or high-level magic).

## Magic system basics (p28-29) {#s-007} (full-fetch)
Tags: [magic, spells]
Know → prepare → cast → forget cycle. Cleric knows *all* cleric spells at or below their level (including rotes, always prepared); Wizard starts knowing cantrips (always prepared) + 3 first-level spells, learns one new spell per level-up, stores known spells in a spellbook. Total levels of prepared (non-cantrip/rote) spells can't exceed caster level+1. Casting is normally the "Cast a Spell" move (full text lives in the Cleric/Wizard playbook chapters, not here): 10+ it works, 7-9 it works but with a complication - one specific 7-9 option is the spell becomes revoked/forgotten (still *known*, but no longer prepared until re-prepared via Commune/Prepare).

## Character change & advancement (p29-31) {#s-008} (full-fetch)
Tags: [xp, advancement]
"Prescriptive and descriptive" framing: changing the sheet should change the fiction and vice versa - advancement isn't a reward mechanism divorced from what's actually happened (example given: a wizard blessed by a deity gains cleric-like Petitions in the fiction *because* of what happened at the table, not by spending a resource). XP sources: exploring/fighting/treasure at session end, fulfilling alignment, resolving a bond, and immediately on any 6-. Level Up move matches [[core-moves]] exactly (subtract level+7 from XP, +1 level, new advanced move, wizard gets a new spell, +1 to a chosen stat max 18, CON increase also bumps max/current HP).

## Bonds (p31-33) {#s-009} (full-fetch)
Tags: [bonds]
A bond resolves when it stops being "a big factor in how you relate" - entirely subjective, negotiated with the other player at end of session (see End of Session, F-003). Unfilled bonds from character creation can be filled in or replaced any time, no XP for doing so. Writing a new bond: pick another character, something relevant from the last session (a place, a shared danger, a treasure), a belief that ties you together, and an action you intend re: that belief.

## Alignment (p33-35) {#s-010} (full-fetch)
Tags: [alignment]
Five alignments: Lawful, Good, Neutral, Chaotic, Evil - each an aspiration, not a straitjacket (two Good characters can still conflict). Most creatures (including most NPCs/monsters) are Neutral. Alignment can change at a decisive narrative moment (with player-explainable reason), or a character can swap which *alignment move* they use while keeping the same alignment (a shift in priority, not belief). **Important structural note**: each class's own playbook chapter lists which alignments it can start with and gives that class's own specific alignment-move text per alignment (this chapter's own bullet lists - e.g. "Uphold the letter of the law," "Ignore danger to aid another" - read as generic illustrative examples of each alignment's flavor, not a universal move list every class shares). Full per-class alignment move text: still needs extraction from the playbook chapters (chunks 2-4).

## Character Creation procedure (p49-57) {#s-012} (full-fetch)
Tags: [chargen]
The 12-step procedure: (1) choose class, one each to start, (2) choose race if the class offers race options - race grants a special move, (3) choose name, (4) choose Look - one item per category, (5) choose stats - either assign the fixed array **16/15/13/12/9/8** to taste (matching moves you want strong), or for more randomness roll 3d6 six times and assign freely, or for maximum randomness roll 3d6 **in STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA order** before even picking a class, (6) derive modifiers (table matches [[core-moves]]), (7) max HP = class base HP + CON score, (8) choose starting moves (some classes have embedded choices; casters choose spellbook/prepared spells now), (9) choose alignment from the class's allowed list, (10) choose starting gear (mind Load), (11) introduce your character to the table (GM asks connective questions), (12) choose bonds - fill in at least one, ideally more, can reuse the same character across multiple bond blanks, leave meaning to be discovered in play rather than over-defining now.

## Basic Moves - remaining moves not already covered in this skill's core-moves.md (p74-83) {#s-013} (full-fetch)
Tags: [moves]
Everything here that overlaps [[core-moves]] (Encumbrance, Make Camp, Take Watch, Undertake a Perilous Journey, End of Session, Level Up, Carouse, Outstanding Warrants) matches what's already documented, confirming that file's accuracy. **New moves not yet in [[core-moves]]**: **Recover** (F-002) and **Supply** (F-005) - note Supply's *actual* trigger is narrower than the fan-guide version already in this skill: it only requires a roll for something *special/non-mundane/beyond what's normally available*; ordinary market-available goods are bought at listed price with no roll at all. **Recruit** (F-004) is an entirely new move (hiring help) not previously captured anywhere in this skill.

**End of Session correction**: the official End of Session move is bond-and-alignment based (resolve a bond + fulfilled-alignment XP + the three yes/no world/foe/treasure questions), not the "Flags" variant - Flags is an alternate/optional sub-system mentioned in the fan GM-screen material already in this skill, not what the core book actually specifies by default.

## Atomic Facts (L2)
- F-001 [s-004] **Multi-attacker damage (official)**: when multiple creatures attack at once, roll damage once using the single highest damage die among the attackers, then add +1 damage per additional attacker beyond the first. Example given in the book: an orkaster (d10+1, ignores armor) + 3 goblins (d6 each) hitting at once → roll 1d10+1 ignoring armor, +3 for the extra attackers, one total.
- F-002 [s-005,s-013] **Recover move**: rest a full day in comfort/safety → recover all HP. Rest 3 days → remove one debility of choice. Under a healer's care → remove a debility every 2 days instead of 3.
- F-003 [s-009] **End of Session move (official)**: resolve one bond (with that player's agreement) → mark XP, write a new bond. Then, if you fulfilled your alignment at least once this session → mark XP. Then as a group answer: did we learn something new & important about the world? / did we overcome a notable monster or enemy? / did we loot a memorable treasure? — mark XP per "yes."
- F-004 [s-011,s-013] **Recruit move**: roll, +1 each if you've made known: generous pay / what you're setting out to do / a share of the find / you have a useful local reputation. 10+: pick of skilled applicants, no penalty for declining. 7-9: settle for someone close to what you wanted, or turn them away. 6-: an ill-suited but influential person insists on joining (foolhardy youth, loose cannon, possible veiled enemy) - bring them and deal with it, or turn them away and take -1 forward to Recruit.
- F-005 [s-013] **Supply move (official wording)**: buying something ordinary/market-available costs listed price, no roll. Only something special/rare/non-mundane calls for roll+CHA: 10+ found at fair price; 7-9 pay more or settle for something close but not exact.
- F-006 [s-012] **Character creation stat methods**: fixed array 16/15/13/12/9/8 (assign to taste) — OR roll 3d6 six times and assign freely — OR (max randomness) roll 3d6 once per stat in strict STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA order, before even choosing a class.
- F-007 [s-005] **Death/resurrection**: 0 HP → immediate Last Breath, no stat bonus. A miss = permanent death by default, but resurrection is always narratively available at a GM-set cost; a new level-1 character is always an option regardless.

## The Bard (p83-90) {#s-014} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, bard]
HP 6+CON, base damage d6, Load 9+STR. Race options: Elf (ask the GM one historical fact on entering an important location) or Human (a hospitality-respecting host takes you in on entering a civilized settlement). Core identity: **Arcane Art** (F-008) is the bard's only spellcasting-flavored move - a performance-triggered utility spell, not a real spell list. **Bardic Lore** (F-009) is a fixed area of expertise granting free true-answer questions about things in that domain, framed as "what song/legend did you hear this in." Alignment options: Good (perform your art to aid someone), Neutral (avoid/defuse conflict), Chaotic (spur others to unplanned decisive action). Full advanced move catalog: F-010.

## The Cleric (p91-104) {#s-015} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, cleric, spells]
HP 8+CON, base damage d6, Load 10+STR. Race options: Dwarf (Commune also grants a stone-only Words of the Unspeaking rote) or Human (pick one wizard spell to cast/be granted as if it were a cleric spell). **This resolves the chunk-1 GAP on Commune/Cast a Spell/spell mechanics** - full move text captured as F-011/F-012. Core identity choices at chargen: a named **Deity**, a **domain** (Healing & Restoration / Bloody Conquest / Civilization / Knowledge & Hidden Things / The Downtrodden & Forgotten / What Lies Beneath), and a religious **precept** that grants a specific **Petition** move (Suffering / Gaining Secrets / Offering / Personal Victory - exact text: F-013). **Turn Undead** (F-014) is a starting move, not gated behind advancement. Alignment options: Good (endanger yourself to heal another), Lawful (endanger yourself following your church's precepts), Evil (harm another to prove your church's superiority). Full advanced move catalog: F-015. **Full Cleric spell catalog** (rotes + levels 1/3/5/7/9, ~30 spells): F-016 - this is dense reference material, kept at near-source grain rather than paraphrased (see digest-skill guidance on convergent L1/L2 grain for catalogs).

## The Druid (p105-112) {#s-016} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, druid]
HP 6+CON, base damage d6, Load 6+STR. Race options: Elf (the Great Forest is always your land in addition to your chosen one), Human (may always shapeshift into domesticated animals in addition to normal options), Halfling (Make Camp heals you and allies +1d6). Core identity: choose a **Land** (11 options: Great Forests, Whispering Plains, Vast Desert, Stinking Mire, River Delta, Depths of the Earth, Sapphire Islands, Open Sea, Towering Mountains, Frozen North, Blasted Wasteland) which bounds what animal shapes you can take, plus a physical **tell** that persists through every shape. **By Nature Sustained**: ignore ration-marking moves entirely. **Spirit Tongue**: understand any animal native/akin to your land. **Shapeshifter** (F-017) is hold-based, not duration-based - GM assigns move(s) tied to the new form, spend hold to trigger them, reverting to natural form when hold runs out (or voluntarily) - this reversion is immediate, mid-fiction, even if the form was flying/climbing/submerged at the time (see F-017 GM note). **Studied Essence**: spend downtime to add a new species to your shapeshift options. Alignment options: Chaotic (destroy a symbol of civilization), Good (help something/someone grow), Neutral (eliminate an unnatural menace). Full advanced move catalog: F-018 - notably includes two moves ("Hunter's Brother," "Stalker's Sister") that let a Druid take a Ranger move directly, an explicit cross-class link worth remembering when a table has both classes.

## Atomic Facts (L2), continued
- F-008 [s-014] **Arcane Art** (Bard, starting move): weave a performance into a basic spell - choose an ally + one effect (heal 1d8; +1d4 forward damage; clear one enchantment from their mind; next aid-assist on them gives +2 instead of +1), then roll+CHA. 10+: effect applies clean. 7-9: effect still applies, but you draw unwanted attention or the magic reverberates to unintended targets (GM's choice).
- F-009 [s-014] **Bardic Lore** (Bard, starting move): choose an expertise (Spells & Magicks / The Dead & Undead / Grand Histories / Bestiary of Unusual Creatures / The Planar Spheres / Legends of Heroes Past / Gods & Their Servants). On first encountering something important covered by it, ask the GM one question, answered truthfully; GM may ask what tale/song you heard it in.
- F-010 [s-014] **Bard advanced moves (names only, by level band)**: 2-5: Healing Song, Vicious Cacophony, It Goes To Eleven, Metal Hurlant, A Little Help From My Friends, Eldritch Tones, Duelist's Parry, Bamboozle, Multiclass Dabbler, Multiclass Initiate. 6-10 (or still-available 2-5 list): Healing Chorus (replaces Healing Song), Vicious Blast (replaces Vicious Cacophony), Unforgettable Face, Reputation, Eldritch Chord (replaces Eldritch Tones), An Ear For Magic, Devious, Duelist's Block (replaces Duelist's Parry), Con (replaces Bamboozle), Multiclass Master. (Exact trigger/effect text for any of these: re-fetch p86-89 if needed - not transcribed here to keep this fact catalog scannable.)
- F-011 [s-015] **Commune** (Cleric, starting move): spend ~an hour in uninterrupted communion with your deity → lose all currently-granted spells, get granted new spells of your choice totaling ≤ your level+1 (none higher level than you), and prepare all rotes free (rotes never count against the limit).
- F-012 [s-015] **Cast a Spell** (Cleric, starting move), roll+WIS: 10+ spell works, deity doesn't revoke it (recastable). 7-9 spell works but choose one: draw unwelcome attention/put yourself in a spot (GM's call) / take -1 ongoing to Cast a Spell until your next Commune / the spell is revoked, uncastable again until you Commune and are re-granted it. Note: maintaining ongoing-tagged spells already stacks a further -1 penalty to this roll (see F-016 spell notes).
- F-013 [s-015] **Cleric Petitions (per precept)**: Suffering (religion preaches suffering's sanctity), Gaining Secrets (cultish/insular religion), Offering (important sacrificial rites), Personal Victory (trial-by-combat religion). Base **Divine Guidance** move: petitioning per your precept grants useful knowledge/a boon tied to your domain, GM's call on specifics.
- F-014 [s-015] **Turn Undead** (Cleric, starting move), roll+WIS: 7+ - while you keep praying/brandishing your holy symbol, no undead may approach within reach. 10+ also - intelligent undead momentarily dazed, mindless undead flee. Any aggression from you breaks the effect; intelligent undead may still harass from range.
- F-015 [s-015] **Cleric advanced moves (names only)**: 2-5: Chosen One, Invigorate, The Scales of Life and Death, Serenity, First Aid, Divine Intervention, Penitent, Empower, Orison for Guidance, Divine Protection, Devoted Healer. 6-10: Anointed (req. Chosen One), Apotheosis, Reaper, Providence (replaces Serenity), Greater First Aid (req. First Aid), Divine Invincibility (replaces Divine Intervention), Martyr (replaces Penitent), Divine Armor (replaces Divine Protection), Greater Empower (replaces Empower), Multiclass Dabbler.
- F-016 [s-015] **Cleric spell catalog** (name — level — [ongoing if tagged] — one-line effect; "ongoing" spells add a further -1 to future Cast a Spell rolls while maintained):
  - Rotes (always prepared, free): *Light* - touched item glows like a colored torch, no fuel/heat, lasts while in your presence. *Sanctify* - consecrate food/water in hand, purified of mundane spoilage. *Guidance* - your deity's symbol gestures a direction/course of action (gesture only).
  - Level 1: *Bless* (ongoing) +1 ongoing to a chosen combatant while they fight. *Cure Light Wounds* heal 1d8 to a touched ally. *Detect Alignment* sense a chosen alignment briefly, GM says what qualifies nearby. *Cause Fear* (ongoing) target fears a nearby object, reacts however they choose. *Magic Weapon* (ongoing) held weapon +1d4 damage. *Sanctuary* ward an area - alerted to malicious action/entry within it; healing there is +1d4. *Speak With Dead* corpse truthfully answers 3 questions from its life/death knowledge.
  - Level 3: *Animate Dead* (ongoing) raise a zombie servant (basic moves only, all stats +1 modifier, 1 HP, plus 1d4 GM-chosen traits from: +2 to one stat / +2 HP per your level / functional brain for complex tasks / doesn't look dead for a day or two); destroyed by damage exceeding its HP or by you ending the spell. *Cure Moderate Wounds* heal 2d8. *Darkness* (ongoing) fill a visible area with supernatural dark. *Resurrection* always possible in principle; GM sets 1+ conditions (time, help needed, cost, sacrifice) - see also F-007's death rules. *Hold Person* target frozen (speech only) until you cast again or leave presence; broken instantly if they take damage.
  - Level 5: *Revelation* deity clarifies the current situation, +1 forward acting on it. *Cure Critical Wounds* heal 3d8. *Divination* clear visions of a named person/place/thing. *Contagion* (ongoing) inflict a disease of your choice. *Words of the Unspeaking* touched non-living object truthfully answers 3 questions. *True Seeing* (ongoing) pierce illusions/hidden things in view. *Trap Soul* imprison a dying creature's soul in a gem (all moves against it +1 while trapped; releasable, not recapturable).
  - Level 7: *Word of Recall* set a recall word; speaking it later teleports you + touching allies back to the casting spot (one location active at a time). *Heal* touched ally heals up to your own max HP. *Harm* touched enemy takes 2d8 (ignores armor), you take 1d6. *Sever* (ongoing) magically remove an appendage, no damage but functional loss. *Mark of Death* runes that kill a named-true-name creature if they read them. *Control Weather* pray for a weather change, arrives within about a day, lasts days.
  - Level 9: *Storm of Vengeance* deity-scale unnatural weather of your choosing. *Repair* undo all effects (damage/poison/disease/magic) of one past event on the target. *Divine Presence* (ongoing) uninvited creatures take +1d10 whenever they take damage in your presence. *Consume Unlife* destroy a touched mindless undead, heal yourself/next ally touched equal to its remaining HP. *Plague* (ongoing) afflict a named settlement with a domain-appropriate plague.
- F-017 [s-016] **Shapeshifter** (Druid, starting move), roll+WIS: 10+ hold 3, 7-9 hold 2, miss hold 1 (plus GM says something). Take the form of any studied/land-native species (stats stay yours, form grants its innate abilities/weaknesses); GM assigns 1+ moves tied to the form; spend 1 hold per use of those moves; reverting to natural form (voluntary or hold exhausted) ends it. **GM note:** hold hitting 0 force-ends the shapeshift *immediately*, mid-fiction, not at the next convenient narrative beat - if the form was flying, climbing, submerged, etc. when hold ran out, the natural form is now doing that (falling, etc.) and the situation resolves from there (Defy Danger, damage, whatever the fiction demands). Don't narrate the form continuing to function past hold 0.

## The Fighter (p113-120) {#s-017} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, fighter]
HP 10+CON (highest base HP of any class), base damage d10 (highest base damage). Load 12+STR. Race options: Dwarf (Parley with CON instead of CHA when sharing a drink), Elf (one chosen weapon type always treated as precise), Halfling (+1 to Defy Danger when leveraging small size), Human (once per battle, reroll any single damage roll - yours or someone else's). Core identity: **Signature Weapon** (F-019) is a heavily customized starting weapon (base type + range + two enhancements + a look) that's narratively unlosable short of deliberate risk. **Bend Bars, Lift Gates** (F-020) is the Fighter's answer to Defy-Danger-style obstacle destruction. Alignment: Good (defend the weaker), Neutral (defeat a worthy opponent), Evil (kill a defenseless/surrendered enemy). Advanced moves catalog: F-021.

## The Paladin (p121-127) {#s-018} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, paladin]
HP 10+CON, base damage d10, Load 12+STR - mechanically as tanky/hard-hitting as the Fighter. **Human-only class** in this edition (single race option: pray and ask "what here is evil?", answered honestly). Core identity: **Lay on Hands** (F-022, heal-by-touch that can backfire onto you on a 7-9), **I Am the Law** (F-023, a Parley-flavored command move backed by divine authority rather than leverage), and **Quest** (F-024) - the signature mechanic: publicly commit to slay/defend/discover something, pick up to two boons (unwavering direction, invulnerability to a chosen thing, a mark of authority, lie-piercing senses, a language-transcending voice, or freedom from hunger/thirst/sleep), and the GM assigns a maintaining vow (Honor/Temperance/Piety/Valor/Truth/Hospitality, each with a specific forbidden or required behavior). Alignment: Lawful (deny mercy to a criminal/unbeliever), Good (endanger yourself for someone weaker) - notably only two alignment options, not three. Two advanced moves (**Divine Favor** / **God Amidst the Wastes** - the latter is actually a Ranger move, see s-019) grant full Cleric-style Commune+Cast a Spell at effective cleric level 1, scaling with future levels - a real spellcasting sidegrade available to non-casters. Advanced moves catalog: F-025.

## The Ranger (p129-136) {#s-019} (full-fetch)
Tags: [playbook, ranger]
HP 8+CON, base damage d8, Load 11+STR. Race options: Elf (auto-succeed, as if 10+, on any Undertake a Perilous Journey role) or Human (Make Camp in dungeon/city costs no ration). Core identity: **Hunt and Track** (tracking-specific Discern-Realities-flavored move), **Called Shot** (F-026, ranged called-shot vs. a helpless/surprised target only), and **Animal Companion** (F-027) - a genuinely detailed sub-character build (species, a Ferocity/Cunning/Armor/Instinct base array with 4 preset options, strengths = ferocity count, trainings = cunning count, weaknesses = instinct count) paired with **Command**, which grants the companion's stats as bonuses across six specific trigger types (attacking the same target, tracking, taking damage, discern realities, parley, being interfered with). Alignment: Chaotic (free someone from bonds), Good (endanger yourself vs. an unnatural threat), Neutral (help an animal/wild spirit). First-advancement-only move **Half-Elven** lets a Ranger retroactively pick up whichever racial move (Elf/Human) they didn't take at chargen. Advanced moves catalog: F-028 - note **"God Amidst the Wastes"** (identical text/effect to the Paladin's "Divine Favor," F-025) is the Ranger's version of the same "become a part-time Cleric" sidegrade.

## Atomic Facts (L2), continued
- F-019 [s-017] **Signature Weapon** (Fighter, starting move): choose a base (Sword/Axe/Hammer/Spear/Flail/Fists, all 2 weight), a range (Hand/Close/Reach), two enhancements from {+1 damage & +1 weight (hooks/spikes); +2 piercing (sharp); precise (perfectly weighted); +1 damage (serrated edges); glows near a chosen creature type; messy+forceful & presumably +weight (huge); an additional range (versatile); -1 weight (well-crafted)}, and a look (Ancient/Unblemished/Ornate/Blood-stained/Sinister). Sidebar rule: absent a clear risk you took on, this weapon narratively cannot permanently leave your possession.
- F-020 [s-017] **Bend Bars, Lift Gates** (Fighter, starting move), roll+STR: 10+ choose 3, 7-9 choose 2, from: doesn't take long / nothing valuable is damaged / not much noise / easily fixable afterward.
- F-021 [s-017] **Fighter advanced moves (names only)**: 2-5: Merciless, Heirloom, Armor Mastery, Improved Weapon, Seeing Red, Interrogator, Scent of Blood, Multiclass Dabbler, Iron Hide, Blacksmith. 6-10: Bloodthirsty (replaces Merciless), Armored Perfection (replaces Armor Mastery), Evil Eye (req. Seeing Red), Taste of Blood (replaces Scent of Blood), Multiclass Initiate (req. Multiclass Dabbler), Steel Hide (replaces Iron Hide), Through Death's Eyes, Eye for Weaponry, Superior Warrior.
- F-022 [s-018] **Lay on Hands** (Paladin, starting move), roll+CHA: 10+ heal 1d8 or cure one disease in a touched ally, no cost to you. 7-9 same effect, but the damage/disease transfers to you instead.

## First Session (p177-184) {#s-023} (full-fetch) — genuinely new content, not previously in this skill
Tags: [gm-advice, chargen]
**Prep**: before session 1, print basic moves + class sheets + cleric/wizard spell sheets + a GM sheet; read the whole book especially GMing/basic moves; get familiar with class moves; read the Fronts rules but don't write any yet. Bring ideas, not a plot - a planned storyline actively conflicts with "play to find out what happens."
**Running character creation**: GM's job is threefold - help, ask questions, take notes (especially on bonds). Set expectations: PCs aren't necessarily friends, just a working team; other spellcasters exist but none are ever really a same-class GM character; coin buys mundane goods only, not magic items; the GM isn't trying to kill the players, the world is just dangerous. **Turn setting questions back on the players** ("Who is the King of Torsea?" → "I don't know - who is it?") rather than answering fiction questions yourself; answer mechanical questions directly. Sharing your own broad-strokes ideas for the world is fine and encouraged, just check the table's actually interested.
**Running the first adventure**: open in medias res on a tense situation (not necessarily prep-required - use a stock opener: dungeon entrance, ambush, spying on guards, on trial before a king), and ask questions about it immediately, ideally questions tied to what the players just established. Session-1-specific goals (F-038): establish/describe, use what they give you, ask questions, leave blanks, note interesting facts, help players learn move triggers, give every character a moment to shine, introduce NPCs without protecting them (a dead NPC is still useful worldbuilding fuel). Watch for unresolved threats mentioned in passing - that's raw material for Fronts.
**After the first session**: rest and let ideas settle before prepping. Front-writing (and any custom moves/monsters) happens *between* sessions 1 and 2, not during session 1 itself.

## Fronts (p185-203) {#s-024} (full-fetch) — expands significantly on [[fronts-and-worldbuilding]]
Tags: [fronts]
**Danger types, impulses, and per-type GM move lists confirmed identical** to what's already in [[fronts-and-worldbuilding]] - no corrections needed there either.
**New structural content**: Fronts are built between sessions, not during play (tweaks happen live, the bulk doesn't). Two front *levels*, not previously distinguished in this skill: an **Adventure Front** is session-scale, tied to one problem, resolved or discarded in a handful of sessions; a **Campaign Front** is the slow-burning throughline spanning the whole game, with bigger/scarier portents. Dangers can migrate between levels in either direction (an unresolved adventure-front danger you like can get promoted to the campaign front; a campaign-front danger can activate down into a specific adventure front). Typical shape: one lightly-detailed campaign front + one or two well-detailed adventure fronts, made right after session 1.
**Concrete front-building step count** (F-039): 2-3 dangers per front (hard cap around 3 - leave room for blanks), pick an Impending Doom per danger, 1-3 Grim Portents for an adventure front vs. 3-5 for a campaign front, 1-3 Stakes questions, a general Cast list.
**Where dangers come from**: named people/monsters that have outgrown "mere monster" status (a cult leader, an ogre chieftain, a goblin tribe as a group) are the easiest start; less obvious candidates (a cursed landscape, a sentient item, a woven-in ancient spell) work just as well; and don't neglect bigger overarching forces (a god, a hidden conspiracy, a prophecy).
**Grim Portents in depth** (F-040): they can be a single event or a chain; often have a logical bad→worse→worst order, but can also be unconnected parallel paths toward the same Impending Doom; check them off as they land, descriptively (something happened in play and this was clearly the next step) or prescriptively (you spend a failed roll/golden opportunity to advance one as a hard move). When *all* of a danger's Grim Portents land, its Impending Doom sets in and the danger resolves (changing the setting meaningfully) - check whether that ripples into your other fronts.
**Stakes, more precisely** (F-041): concrete and important, not vague mood questions - a good stakes question, once resolved, means things are permanently different. Once written, it's genuinely out of your hands to just decide - you have to play to find out, same as everything else in the game.
**Resolving a front**: a single-dungeon adventure front usually dissolves cleanly (dangers killed/redeemed/overcome); leftover unresolved pieces can migrate to the campaign front. Campaign fronts resolve slowly in pieces over the whole game. When any front resolves, pause and ask what changed in the world as a result (even from Grim Portents that fired), and whether any cast members should move, get promoted, or get reduced.
**Running multiple adventure fronts at once**: fine and often desirable (parallel, unrelated Impending Dooms create real prioritization choices for the players) - just watch your own bandwidth past 3-4 concurrent fronts.
**Worked example - "The Opening of the White Gate"** (F-042): a second full worked front, more compact than the fan-supplement Great Wyrm example already in [[fronts-and-worldbuilding]] - useful as an alternate template, especially for a "planar incursion" style front rather than a "monster menaces a region" style front.

- F-053 [s-037] **Equipment tag glossary**: *Applied* - only works carefully applied to a person/their food or drink. *Awkward* - unwieldy. *+Bonus* - a specific situational modifier. *n coins* - price; a "-Charisma" cost subtracts the haggler's raw CHA score (not modifier). *Dangerous* - GM may freely invoke consequences of careless handling. *Ration* - edible. *Requires* - works poorly/not at all without meeting a requirement. *Slow* - takes minutes+. *Touch* - applied by touching the target's skin. *Two-handed* - needs both hands. *n weight* - counts against Load; unlisted weight = not meant to be carried; 100 standard coins = 1 weight (gems/art may differ). *Worn* - must be worn to use. *n Uses* - limited uses. *n Ammo* - counts as ammo stock, not individual arrows. *Forceful* - can knock back/off feet. *+n Damage* - add n to damage dealt. *Ignores Armor* - target's armor doesn't reduce this damage. *Messy* - destructively rips apart. *n Piercing* - subtracts n from the target's armor for that attack. *Precise* - Hack & Slash with DEX instead of STR. *Reload* - takes more than a moment to reset after use. *Stun* - deals stun damage instead of normal. *Thrown* - once thrown it's gone (can't choose to spend ammo instead on a 7-9 Volley). *n Armor* - only the single highest-value armor item counts, non-stacking. *+n Armor* - stacks with other armor. *Clumsy* - -1 ongoing while worn, cumulative across multiple clumsy items.

## Atomic Facts (L2), continued
- F-056 [s-039] **Order Hirelings move (exact wording)**: hirelings do as told so long as it's not obviously dangerous/degrading/stupid and their cost is met. When an order puts them in real danger/degradation/craziness, roll+Loyalty: 10+ stand firm and comply; 7-9 comply now, return later with serious demands - meet them or they quit on the worst possible terms.
- F-057 [s-040] **Direct Conversion cheat-sheet** (translating a foreign monster stat block into DW): Damage - single die+bonus(≤10) unchanged; same-size multiple dice → roll all, take highest; different-size multiple dice → roll only the largest, take its result. HP - Hit-Dice-based → max of first HD + 1 per additional HD; flat HP with no HD → divide by 4. Armor - average AC→1, low AC→0, high AC→2 (3 if defense-specialist), nearly invulnerable→4, +1 more if the defense is explicitly magical. Moves/Instinct - derive directly from the original's special abilities/attack list.
